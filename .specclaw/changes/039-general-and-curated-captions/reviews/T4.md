## Findings

### [NOTE] src/lib/captions/actionTargets.ts:101 and src/lib/captions/rules.ts (composeFacebookCaption) — Correctness (edge)

**Problem:** `const text = row.channel === 'FACEBOOK' ? composeFacebookCaption(field.text, extra?.website ?? null) : field.text` can add up to 2048 characters to a body that the extractor held to the 2000-character `hardLimit`. The composed text may then exceed 2000. The prompt says "At most 2000 characters" without counting the link. A user who edits such a caption would hit the edit route's length limit and have to shorten it.
**Suggestion:** Rare in practice (the body is 40 to 120 words). Either cap the body at `hardLimit - website.length - 2` in the extractor, or accept and say so in the design.

### [NOTE] src/lib/captions/extract.ts:~118 — Security (coverage)

**Problem:** `findLinks` matches `http(s)://` and `www.`. A model reply with a bare domain ("acme.example/offer") passes as text. It cannot inject markup, and the model never sees the real URL, so the risk is a wrong link in the draft, not a security hole.
**Suggestion:** None required; the `link-missing` check and the author's review cover it.

### [NOTE] src/lib/brandkit/website.ts — Naming (comment)

**Problem:** The header says "kept as typed after the trim". Good; but the PATCH route's inline comment says "only an https URL is stored" while a literal IP such as `https://127.0.0.1` also passes (dotted host). Harmless: no fetch.
**Suggestion:** None.

## Verdict Rationale

The security-sensitive points hold: a strict https-only parser, admin-only writes, a URL that never enters the prompt, a model link that is refused, and no internal fields in the MCP output. Compose is single-shot and idempotent on regenerate and undo, the migrations respect the enum rule and the down script fails closed with a full upgrade-path scenario. Only edge-case notes remain.
